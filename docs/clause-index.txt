# The clause index (`openai-chat` is the OpenAPI document pinned in docs/schemas/openai-chat.md; `openai-chat-sse` is the stream framing, which that document does not specify: it is taken from the vendor's streaming description and OpenRouter's documented examples, always `policy`): the parts of each pinned convention (docs/schemas/) that this library reads, decides by policy, or deliberately ignores. DATA, extracted from the pinned schema by reading it.
# Line: <convention> <clause-id> <read|policy|ignored> <what the clause says and what the library does>.
# `read`: the library decodes it as the schema says. `policy`: the library decides something the schema leaves open (stated in the clause text). `ignored`: present in the schema, not read.
# The obligation ledger (VbmLlmProofs/Ledger.lean) tags a claim with `convention:clause-id`; the ledger gate fails on a tag that is not in this index, and reports the share of read and policy clauses
# that a proved or partial claim tags (a coverage of traceability, never a statement that the clause was stated faithfully).
